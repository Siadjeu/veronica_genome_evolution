# Figure 3
## Targeted chromosome relationships in diploids and tetraploids
Synteny-optimized chromosome comparisons were used to contrast chromosome
relationships among diploid genomes and between diploid and tetraploid
genomes.
(A) VSCU–VSER, diploid–diploid comparison.
(B) VSCU–VPAN, diploid–diploid comparison.
(C) VSCU–VPER, diploid–tetraploid comparison.
(D) VPAN–VPER, independent diploid–tetraploid comparison.
Chromosomes are shown as numbered bars.
Blue links represent mapped full-anchor synteny pairs, whereas red links
connect chromosome pairs participating in multiple strong-partner
relationships.
The diploid–diploid comparisons predominantly show simpler chromosome
correspondences, whereas comparisons involving the tetraploid VPER show
increased multisyntenic relationships, with individual diploid chromosomes
corresponding to multiple regions of the tetraploid genome.
