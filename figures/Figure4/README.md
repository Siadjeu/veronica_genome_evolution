# Figure 4

## Ks distributions of syntenic blocks

Kernel-density distributions of synonymous substitutions per synonymous
site (Ks) were examined for syntenic blocks identified within and between
genomes.

**(A)** Combined distributions for the nine self-comparisons (PMAJ, VPAN,
VSCU, VANA, VARV, VPER, VSER, VTRI and VVER), together with the primary
between-genome comparisons PMAJ–VSCU, VSCU–VANA and VSCU–VPER.

**(B)** Additional independent between-genome comparisons VSCU–VSER,
VSCU–VPAN and VPAN–VPER.

The x-axis represents synonymous substitutions per synonymous site (Ks),
and the y-axis represents the kernel density of syntenic blocks.

The additional diploid–diploid comparisons provide controls for interpreting
lower-Ks components associated with species divergence. Older Ks components
shared among multiple comparisons are evaluated as deeper duplication and
divergence signals but are not, by themselves, considered proof of an
ancestral *Veronica*-specific whole-genome duplication.
